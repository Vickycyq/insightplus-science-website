ABOUT INSIGHTPLUS SCIENCE

A Flexible Supply Partner for Laboratory Consumables

INSIGHTPLUS SCIENCE is dedicated to becoming a specialist small-batch, flexible supply chain partner for laboratory consumables. We help research, healthcare, education, and industrial laboratories source the products they need through responsive sourcing and coordinated supply.

Our product range covers General Plasticware; Glassware; Anatomy, Pathology & Clinical Supplies; Filtration Consumables; Crucibles & Metal Consumables; Liquid Handling Consumables; and Electrochemistry Supplies. We support mixed orders, consolidated shipments, fast sample evaluation, and low-MOQ customization for packaging, lot labels, sterilization, and individual packing.
